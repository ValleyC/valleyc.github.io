Ruogu Chen

PhD Candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering

I am a PhD candidate at the University of Alberta, supervised by Prof. Jie Han. My research focuses on machine learning for electronic design automation (EDA), particularly chip placement.

I study how routing and timing feedback can guide placement decisions beyond early-stage proxies. My work explores differentiable prediction, language-model-guided objective design, and diffusion models for generating and refining layouts. I also develop structure-aware generative methods for combinatorial optimization.

Previously, I received my BEng in Electrical and Communication Engineering from City University of Hong Kong in 2022, with First Class Honours.
